### Supported bounded observations

An open direct `blocks` prerequisite excluded the target from native ready. Closing that prerequisite admitted the target, while the closed dependency remained visible through show/dependency reads. A new open blocker added after claim remained visible under `in_progress`; claim does not seal eligibility, and further effects must refuse after this change.

The observed open task child of an open epic was ready when its direct blocker was closed and the epic's downward dependencies were an empty array. This qualifies only a pure structural epic-container case. It does not establish general parent/ancestor readiness. A task with `status:deferred` and nonempty future `defer_until` was excluded from ready.

Repeated `tower-stage` transitions returned distinct native event IDs. Direct bounded readback of each ID preserved the actual `created_by` actor, original task `parent`, parent-child dependency and exact host reason reference/digest in `description`. This is the selected native append/readback interface; audit history and state labels cannot replace it. A successful bounded write, known EOF/cleanup and exact live host bindings remain prerequisites. A timeout after a possible write stays unknown, with no retry or speculative fallback. Append-notes was not qualified.

Claim timestamps showed a five-minute lease. Owner heartbeat advanced `heartbeat_at` and `lease_expires_at` by one second in this observation; the foreign actor was rejected. Installed public heartbeat help documents node-local ephemeral lease storage, with no Dolt commit or history write. That statement is help-backed, not a direct database inspection. Lease data is not durable execution/acceptance evidence. No cadence, cancellation callback, scheduler, reclaim or recovery behavior is implemented or qualified here. The amended design plans fixed 60-second renewal of current live claims on the serialized coordinator lane, with a cheap memory-only cancellation callback. Expiry, owner loss or an unknown heartbeat outcome must cancel/fence without replay or revival. This remains planned; configurable health/budgets belong to .4.

### Refused and unresolved semantics

Complex gates, waits-for, other dependency kinds, malformed/missing/unknown kinds or statuses, non-epic/multiple structural parents, parents with dependencies and unverified ancestors are unsupported. Refuse their effects rather than treating them as empty blockers or implementing an application DAG evaluator. The positive cases do not establish that an arbitrary issue lacks unexposed gate semantics; dependent implementation must prove the strict supported subset from qualified native facts or refuse it.

Broad `ready --claim` is refused. Public help has no exact-ID selector and these observations do not prove a policy-reserved complete possible match set. Do not invent `ready --id`, manufacture label filters, claim then undo an unauthorized result, or equate readiness with authorization.

Unknown write, owner, cleanup, output completeness or event-readback outcome stops affected effects and preserves the existing fence. No automatic replay, unclaim, reassignment, prior-session adoption, lease-expiry recovery or interpretation of copied JSON is authorized. Structurally valid source/model JSON, closed native events and task status alone never grant ownership, accepted/done, closure or slot release. Full history enumeration, overflow, alternate types/gates, other no-op behavior and cross-machine enforcement remain unqualified; the same-state/new-reason non-appending case is documented separately above.
